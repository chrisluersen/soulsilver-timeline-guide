SoulSilver · Timeline Guide

Follow the timeline — everything you need, when you need it

Each phase bundles every decision: what to catch, what to grab, what to buy, which moves to keep, which Gym to sweep, and where to save. No jumping between sections — just follow it in order. Verified against Serebii + Bulbapedia (2026-08).

🔄 Trade Team — the full-power six

The flagship comp. Alakazam + Gengar come online via the Union Room trade (Phase 2.5, ~30–45 min, one-time). Highest ceiling in the game — two S-tier special sweepers and no moveset compromises. Pick this if you can spare one setup session.

Team: Typhlosion · Gyarados · Alakazam · Heracross · Gengar · Lapras
Payoff: S/S/A/A/A/B — the strongest six Johto offers
Setup: one 30–45 min Union Room trade (see P2.5)
Downside: none real — trades are one-time and reusable

🚫 Zero-Trade — no Union Room, no problem

Same timeline, zero trading. Kadabra stays Kadabra (B-tier, lower SpA) and Ampharos (Route 32 Mareep, Electric) replaces Gengar. B-tier ceiling but zero setup — nothing to unlock, no second emulator instance, Phase 2.5 becomes optional.

Team: Typhlosion · Gyarados · Kadabra · Heracross · Ampharos · Lapras
Payoff: S/S/B/A/B/B — every mon catchable without trading
Setup: none — skip Phase 2.5 entirely
Downside: Kadabra's lower SpA; Ampharos lacks Levitate + Ghost coverage

⚡ Speedrun — Any% Glitchless

Goal: beat Lance as fast as possible (WR ~1:57 Voltorb-Flip route / ~3:42 manipless). Cyndaquil solos the early game, then a level-50 Magikarp trick hands you an instant Gyarados that carries to the E4. Skip everything optional.

Starter: Cyndaquil (best early Fire; Fire Blast from Dept Store)
Carry: Route-43 level-50 Magikarp → Gyarados
Skip: trades, Lapras, Heracross, most TMs/items
Buy: Fire Blast TM + repels at Goldenrod

💀 Nuzlocke — Standard Rules

One catch per area (dupes clause), fainted = dead (boxed forever), gift/shiny/static are freebies, no trading (except evo-by-trade). You take what you get — prioritize bulk + coverage, box HM slaves, and overlevel. The freebies (Red Gyarados, Lapras) are your MVPs.

Top catches: Cyndaquil, Mareep→Ampharos, Geodude, Nidoran, Wooper
Freebies: Red Gyarados, Lapras (Fri), Eevee, Togepi
Avoid relying on: Abra (teleports), Gastly (low-tier)
Rule: fainted = dead. Overlevel before every gym.

Phase 1 — New Bark → Violet City (lv 5 → ~12)

▾

ROUTE The intro

Pick Cyndaquil in Elm's Lab, do the Route 29 tutorial battle, explore Route 30/31, arrive at Violet City. Grind Cyndaquil to ~10 on wild Pokémon before Falkner.

CATCH Your first two

  • Mon Cyndaquil — starter, minute 1. → Quilava at lv 14 (end of this phase / start of Phase 2)
  • Mon Gastly — Sprout Tower 1F–3F, night only (85% encounter). Catch ONE. → Haunter at 25 (Phase 3)

GRAB On the way

  • TM TM70 Flash — Sprout Tower Elder, free after clearing the tower

MOVES Level-up picks

  • Cynda Tackle → Leer → Smokescreen(lv6) → Ember(lv10) → Quick Attack(lv13). Keep Ember — your only Fire STAB until Flame Wheel at 20.
  • Gastly Hypnosis → Lick → Spite → Mean Look(lv8) → Curse(lv12). Hypnosis helps catching; Mean Look prevents run.

GYM Falkner (Flying)

Cyndaquil's Ember 2×s his Pidgey + Pidgeotto. Easy.

SAVE First durable save

In-game save at Violet Poké Center — this creates a real .sav file. Do not leave Violet without it. After saving, back up to OneDrive — see the Save Protocol reference.

ROUTE Rush, don't grind

Pick Cyndaquil, sprint Route 29/30/31. Don't catch anything — every ball thrown is time lost. Falkner: Ember 2×s.

SKIP No Gastly, no TM70 detour

Skip Sprout Tower's Gastly hunt entirely. Grab TM70 Flash only if you're already there (it's free after the Elder).

CATCH Your first area catches

Cyndaquil + first encounters on Route 29/30/31. Gastly is low-tier in nuzlocke (VERY LOW) — don't count on it. Prioritize anything with bulk.

GYM Falkner

Cyndaquil Ember. Overlevel to ~12 first — nuzlocke = never walk in even.

03

Phase 2 — Azalea → Goldenrod (lv 12 → ~20)

▾

ROUTE Through Ilex Forest to Azalea → Goldenrod

Get Headbutt in Ilex Forest from the guy after the Farfetch'd quest. Headbutt the trees in Azalea Town for Heracross (30%, lv 2–5). Beat Bugsy (Quilava rolls him). Grab Charcoal from the kiln owner. Push to Goldenrod — the big city.

CATCH Team slots 3 and 4

  • Mon Heracross — Azalea trees, 30% encounter (lv 2–5). No evolution. Never misses.
  • Mon Abra — Goldenrod Game Corner, 200 coins (lv 15). → Kadabra at 16 (evolve immediately). → Alakazam via trade (Phase 2.5).

GRAB Items you need here

  • Held Charcoal — Azalea Charcoal Kiln, after Bugsy. → Typhlosion (Fire +20%)
  • TM TM52 Focus Blast — Goldenrod Dept Store 5F, 5,500₽. Buy it now. → Typhlosion / Gengar coverage later.
  • TM TM27 Return — Dept Store Sunday lady if your lead Pokémon has high friendship. → Gyarados later.

BUY Goldenrod Dept Store (Floors 2–5)

  • Buy Super Potion ×5+ — 700₽ (2F) — workhorse heal for this whole phase
  • Buy Great Ball ×10 — 600₽ (2F) — Heracross + Abra + Lapras tier
  • Buy Ultra Ball ×5 — 1,200₽ (2F) — Gyarados + stubborn catches
  • Buy Super Repel ×5 — 500₽ (2F) — caves stop being a slog
  • Buy Escape Rope ×2 — 550₽ (2F) — Union Cave + Victory Road
  • Skip 3F X-items (not needed), 4F vitamins (9,800₽ — expensive, skip in run), 6F drinks

MOVES Level-up picks

  • Quilava Evolve Cynda→Quilava at lv 14. Flame Wheel(lv20) replaces Ember — much stronger Fire STAB.
  • Heracross Endure(lv—) → Fury Attack(lv7) → Aerial Ace(lv13) → Brick Break(lv19). Brick Break is your main Fighting move until Close Combat at 37.
  • Kadabra Abra→Kadabra at lv16 (learns Confusion on evolution). Then Disable(lv18). Confusion carries through this phase.
  • Gastly Curse(lv12) → Night Shade(lv15) → Confuse Ray(lv19). Confuse Ray is your utility.

GYM Bugsy → Whitney

Bugsy (Bug): Quilava rolls him — Fire STAB 2×s everything.

Whitney (Normal): first real wall. Heracross Brick Break (lv19) or Kadabra Confusion. Rollout Miltank is the threat — Brick Break breaks through.

ROUTE Straight to Goldenrod

Headbutt for Heracross only if a quick tree pops one (optional). Beat Bugsy with Quilava. Buy Fire Blast TM (Dept Store) + repels — this is the run's power spike.

GYM Whitney

Overleveled Quilava Fire Blast 2HKOs Miltank. Don't waste time on Heracross/Kadabra setup.

CATCH The good early mons

Mareep (Route 32) → Ampharos is a top-tier nuzlocke catch. Geodude walls Bugsy. Heracross (Azalea trees) is solid if it pops. Nidoran/Wooper are great if you see them.

GYM Bugsy → Whitney

Bugsy: Geodude/Quilava. Whitney: overlevel + don't let Miltank set up Rollout — switch to reset it.

Phase 2.5 — The Trade Setup (lv ~17–22)

▾

⚠️ One-time, ~30–45 min. See Trade Setup Reference for full walkthrough.

Goldenrod's Union Room (2nd floor Pokécom Center). You trade Kadabra→Alakazam and Haunter→Gengar using a second melonDS instance. Do both in one session.

RESULT Team power spike

  • Alakazam S-tier. 135 SpA + 120 Speed with Calm Mind later. Entire back half revolves around this Pokémon.
  • Gengar A-tier. 130 SpA, Levitate (Ground immunity). Shadow Ball at 33 for free.

SAVE Save state after both trades complete

Savestate slot 1 (F1) — locks in the team.

SKIP No trade needed — this phase is optional

Zero-trade comp: skip the Union Room entirely. Kadabra stays Kadabra (B-tier, same Psychic moves, lower stats), and the Gengar slot is filled by Ampharos instead — grab a Mareep on Route 32 (early, catches up fast) and evolve it through Flaaffy to Ampharos naturally.

CATCH The Ampharos slot

  • Mon Mareep — Route 32, ~lv 6, 20%/10% encounter. Evolves Flaaffy → Ampharos. No trade, Electric STAB. Grind it alongside the team so it hits the mid-game power curve.

RESULT Honest trade-off

  • Trade Alakazam S-tier + Gengar A-tier > Kadabra B-tier + Ampharos B-tier. You lose the two trade power spikes (135/120 Alakazam, 130 SpA Ghost sweeps).
  • Gain Zero setup, no second instance, no 30–45 min grind to Goldenrod. Electric coverage you wouldn't otherwise have.
  • E4 Ampharos handles Will's Xatu + Lance's Flyers (Electric 2× on Flying); Kadabra keeps Psychic for Koga/Bruno. Just less raw power than the trade versions.

SKIP No trades

Speedrun never touches the Union Room. Keep moving.

SKIP No trades

Nuzlocke = no trading (except evo-by-trade). Kadabra stays Kadabra; Ampharos fills the slot. Abra is unreliable (Teleport) — don't plan around it.

Phase 3 — Ecruteak → Olivine City (lv ~20 → 28)

▾

ROUTE Ecruteak → National Park → Union Cave → Olivine

Beat Morty (Alakazam sweeps). Kimono Girl event at the Dance Theater unlocks Surf. Then Route 38/39, National Park (Quick Claw), Olivine.

CATCH Slot 6 — Lapras (Friday only)

  • Mon Lapras — Union Cave B2F, Friday only. Enter from Violet City side, surf to the pond at the bottom of B2F. lv 20, one per week. Don't KO it or leave the room — gone until next Friday.

GRAB The mid-game haul

  • Held Quick Claw — National Park (Route 35 side) → Heracross (speed insurance)
  • HM HM06 Rock Smash — Hiker on Route 36
  • TM TM30 Shadow Ball — Morty's gym reward (free!) → Alakazam or Gengar
  • Held Spell Tag — Ecruteak City → Gengar (Ghost +20%)
  • HM HM03 Surf — Dance Theater, Kimono Girl after the Rocket grunt (post-Morty)
  • TM TM57 Charge Beam — Olivine shore, right of the lighthouse → Alakazam
  • Free Moomoo Milk — Miltank farm on Route 38/39 → buy a stack, cheaper than Potions

MOVES Level-up picks

  • Alakazam Psybeam(lv24) → Reflect(lv28) → Recover(lv30) → Calm Mind(lv36) next phase. Keep Recover — it's your only reliable healing.
  • Haunter→Gengar Sucker Punch(lv22) → evolves to Haunter at lv25 (learns Shadow Punch) → Payback(lv28) → Shadow Ball(lv33) next phase. Shadow Ball is FREE — don't waste a TM on it.
  • Lapras (caught at lv 20 — already knows Water Pulse/Body Slam). Next: Rain Dance(lv22) → Perish Song(lv27) → Ice Beam(lv32) next phase. Ice Beam is also FREE.
  • Quilava Swift(lv31) coming up — filler. Lava Plume at 35 next phase.

GYM Morty → Chuck → Jasmine

Morty (Ghost): Alakazam Confusion/Psychic — Ghost/Poison typing makes Psychic 2×. Clean sweep.

Chuck (Fighting): Alakazam / Gyarados (if you have it early). Return from the Sunday lady helps. Primeape → Poliwrath (Water/Fighting — watch for Surf).

Jasmine (Steel): Typhlosion / Quilava — Fire STAB 2×s her whole team (Steelix + 2 Magnemite).

ROUTE Morty → Surf

Morty: Quilava Fire Blast. Kimono Girl → Surf. Skip Lapras (Friday wait + setup is too slow for a run).

CATCH Lapras freebie (Friday)

Lapras (Union Cave B2F, Friday) is a gift-tier freebie — huge bulk + Ice Beam for Lance. Don't miss the Friday window.

GYM Morty → Chuck → Jasmine

Morty: Alakazam/Kadabra Psychic. Chuck: watch DynamicPunch — bring Full Heals. Jasmine: Fire STAB.

Phase 4 — Cianwood → Lake of Rage (lv ~28 → 35)

▾

ROUTE Cianwood → Mahogany → Rocket HQ → Lake of Rage → Pryce

Sail to Cianwood, beat Chuck for the badge + Fly. SecretPotion → Olivine, cure Amphy, Jasmine gives badge. Then Route 42 → Mahogany: Rocket Hideout (Whirlpool HM). Clear Team Rocket, get the free shiny Gyarados at Lake of Rage.

CATCH Your physical sweeper arrives

  • Mon Gyarados (already evolved — free shiny, Lake of Rage, lv 30). Add to team immediately. Intimidate + Ice Fang at 32 makes it useful from minute 1.

GRAB The back-half power items

  • HM HM02 Fly — Cianwood, after Chuck (fast travel unlocked)
  • Slave Fly + Cut slave — the free Farfetch’d (Ilex Forest quest, Phase 2) carries HM02 Fly + Cut in one box slot. Swap in, Fly, swap out. See HM Strategy.
  • Slave HM02 Fly — Smeargle handles it (see HM Strategy). No team member touches an HM.
  • Slave HM02 Fly — Furret covers the other HMs; grab the free Farfetch’d (Ilex) for Fly. See HM Strategy.
  • Slave HM02 Fly — Pidgey (Route 29, minute 1) → Pidgeot carries it. Box mon.
  • Fly — Dodrio (in the team) covers HM02 — no slave needed. See HM Strategy.
  • Slave — no Fly slave in this comp. You walk. See HM Strategy.
  • HM HM04 Strength — Hiker on Route 42
  • TM TM65 Shadow Claw — Route 42 → Gyarados (physical Ghost coverage)
  • HM HM05 Whirlpool — Lance, Mahogany Rocket Hideout (for Lugia later)
  • TM TM36 Sludge Bomb — Route 43 guard, after Team Rocket leaves Mahogany → Gengar
  • Held Choice Specs — Lake of Rage (Surf needed, north-east patch) → Alakazam (Sp. Atk ×1.5)

BUY Cianwood Pharmacy (the only Hyper Potion shop in Johto)

  • Buy Hyper Potion ×5 — 1,200₽ each — regular marts don't stock these until badge 5
  • Buy Revive ×3 — 1,500₽ — stocked nowhere else this early; grab before Chuck/Jasmine
  • Buy Full Heal ×3 — 600₽ — status is the real killer vs Chuck (Primeape's DynamicPunch)

MOVES Level-up picks — the team comes online

  • Typhlosion Lava Plume(lv35) → evolve at 36 to Typhlosion! → Flamethrower(lv42) next phase. Lava Plume is the mid-game power spike. Keep it until Flamethrower.
  • Gengar Shadow Ball(lv33) — free, learn naturally. This is your main special Ghost move for the rest of the game.
  • Lapras Ice Beam(lv32) — free, learn naturally. This is the Clair + Lance killer.
  • Gyarados Ice Fang(lv32) — free! Then Aqua Tail(lv35), Rain Dance(lv38). Dragon Dance(lv44) next phase.
  • Alakazam Calm Mind(lv36) — free setup. Then Psychic(lv40) next phase.
  • Heracross Close Combat(lv37) — free. This replaces Brick Break. Best Fighting move in the game.

⚠️ Don't waste Game Corner coins

Every move above is learned free by level-up — never spend coins on Flamethrower/Psychic/Ice Beam/Shadow Ball. The only TMs worth buying are TM52 Focus Blast (Dept Store, already in your bag) and TM24 Thunderbolt (Game Corner, optional for Gengar/Lapras).

CATCH The level-50 Magikarp trick

On Route 43, surf with a repel + high-level lead to force a level-50 Magikarp → one Rare Candy → instant Gyarados. This is the run's carry. Grab Strength (Route 42).

GYM Pryce

Gyarados or Typhlosion. Bench Gyarados if it's 4× Ice-weak — use Typhlosion.

CATCH Red Gyarados freebie

Red Gyarados (Lake of Rage) is the nuzlocke MVP — guaranteed, bulky, Intimidate. Add it immediately.

GYM Pryce

Bench Gyarados (4× Ice-weak). Typhlosion/Heracross Close Combat.

GYM Chuck → Jasmine → Pryce

Pryce (Ice): Typhlosion or Heracross. Gyarados is 4× weak to Ice — bench it for this fight. Heracross Close Combat 2×s his Dewgong+Piloswine.

Phase 5 — Blackthorn → Victory Road (lv ~35 → 45+)

▾

ROUTE Ice Path → Blackthorn → Clair → Victory Road

Ice Path (Waterfall HM + Never-Melt Ice). Beat Clair's gym — Lapras Ice Beam sweeps. Then the E4 gauntlet: Victory Road holds TM26 Earthquake and levels.

GRAB Last items before the E4

  • HM HM07 Waterfall — Ice Path dungeon (required to enter the E4 building + Lugia cave)
  • Held Never-Melt Ice — Ice Path → Lapras (Ice +20%)
  • TM TM59 Dragon Pulse — Clair's reward, Blackthorn Gym
  • TM TM26 Earthquake — Victory Road (pick up pre-E4!) → Gyarados (physical coverage for the E4)

MOVES Final moveset milestones

  • Typhlosion Flamethrower(lv42) — replaces Lava Plume (stronger, more accurate). Eruption(lv57) is post-game.
  • Alakazam Psychic(lv40) — replaces Confusion/Psybeam. Debuffs Sp. Def 10% — sets up Koga and Will to crumble.
  • Gyarados Dragon Dance(lv44) — +1 Atk +1 Speed. Setup one Dragon Dance → sweep. This is the E4 strategy.

GYM Clair (Dragon)

Lapras Ice Beam — her Kingdra (Dragon/Water) is 2× weak to Ice, and Lapras has 130 base HP to tank. Give Lapras Never-Melt Ice from Ice Path. Clair gives TM59 Dragon Pulse — nice to have, not essential.

SAVE Before Victory Road

Save state (F1) at Blackthorn Poké Center. Victory Road has tough trainers — don't lose progress. In-game save after every major milestone.

ROUTE Gyarados solos

Strength/Surf/Waterfall + Dragon Dance. Clair: Gyarados. Grab TM26 Earthquake (Victory Road) for coverage.

ROUTE Overlevel before Clair

Clair: Lapras Ice Beam. Grind to ~40+ before Victory Road — nuzlocke deaths are permanent, don't risk it.

Phase 6 — Elite Four (lv ~45, E4 levels 42–50)

▾

COUNTERS Your six vs the gauntlet

  • Will Psychic — Gengar Shadow Ball (Ghost 2× Psychic). Xatu is also Psychic/Flying — Gengar handles it.
  • Will Psychic — Ampharos Thunderbolt (Electric 2× Xatu). Xatu is Psychic/Flying. Less coverage than Gengar, still works.
  • Koga Poison — Alakazam Psychic (2× Poison). His Muk has high Sp. Def — Calm Mind first.
  • Koga Poison — Kadabra Psychic (2× Poison). Lower SpA — level up first / pack X-items.
  • Bruno Fighting — Alakazam Psychic / Gyarados Waterfall. His Onix are weak to Water.
  • Bruno Fighting — Kadabra Psychic / Gyarados Waterfall. His Onix are weak to Water.
  • Karen Dark — Heracross Close Combat (Fighting 2× Dark). Gengar Shadow Ball for Houndoom.
  • Karen Dark — Heracross Close Combat (Fighting 2× Dark). Ampharos + Gyarados for Houndoom.
  • Lance Dragon — Lapras Ice Beam (4× his Dragonite). Gyarados Ice Fang for backup.

Lance's team: Gyarados → Dragonite ×3 → Charizard → Aerodactyl. All Flying-typed. Ice 4× on Dragonite, 2× on everything else. Lapras + Ice Beam + Choice Specs Alakazam backup = clean.

OPTIONAL Lugia swap

Grab Lugia (Whirl Islands, lv 45, needs Whirlpool) before the E4 and swap in for Typhlosion — Fire is dead weight in this fight. Lugia with Surf/Aeroblast covers Will+Koga+Bruno better.

COUNTERS Gyarados sweeps

Dragon Dance once, then sweep. Will/Karen lead with Confuse Ray — be careful with multiple DDs. Lance: Gyarados Ice Fang / Waterfall.

COUNTERS Use your freebies

Lance: Lapras Ice Beam (4× Dragonite). Gyarados backup. Overlevel to ~45+ and heal between every E4 member.

🎮 Game-Design Fundamentals — why the team works

STAB & type matchups

STAB = Same-Type Attack Bonus: ×1.5 when move type matches user type. Type effectiveness (2× / ½× / 0×) is the single biggest damage multiplier — one correct type hit beats any stat lead.

Typhlosion's Fire moves get STAB; Fire on Grass/Bug 2×s.

Physical vs Special — Gen 4 split

Gen 4 was the first generation where every move has its own category (not the type). Special: Shadow Ball, Flamethrower, Ice Beam, Psychic, Surf, Thunderbolt, Sludge Bomb. Physical: Close Combat, Waterfall, Ice Fang, Return, Brick Break, Aerial Ace.

Gengar (130 SpA) uses Special Shadow Ball; Heracross (125 Atk) uses Physical Close Combat — match move category to the stat.

Natures, EVs, IVs

Don't reset for them in a story run. Prefer Modest (+SpA −Atk) on special attackers, Adamant (+Atk −SpA) on physical ones. Levels + type matchups carry a playthrough.

Abilities

Guts (Heracross): ×1.5 Atk when statused — burn makes it hit harder. Intimidate (Gyarados): drops foe's Atk on switch-in. Levitate (Gengar): immune to Ground.
