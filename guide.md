## HM Strategy — pick your slave

Your six's optimal sets already carry two HMs for free: **Surf** (Lapras) and **Waterfall** (Gyarados) — no moveset damage. The other six HMs (Cut, Fly, Strength, Whirlpool, Rock Smash, Rock Climb) would cost a team member a slot. The live HTML has an **HM strategy** tab bar with all six comps; the two winners:

- **Smeargle — one slave, all 8 HMs (zero moveset damage).** Learns every HM via Sketch (Ruins of Alph, post-Violet; Heart Scales to re-learn). Cut/Fly/Strength/Whirlpool/Rock Smash/Rock Climb → Smeargle; Surf → Lapras; Waterfall → Gyarados. Only comp where no team member touches an HM.
- **Furret + Farfetch'd — the easy pick.** Sentret (Route 29, minute 1) → Furret at 15 covers Cut/Strength/Whirlpool/Rock Smash. Free Ilex Farfetch'd covers Fly + Cut. Zero moveset damage on the six; only gap is Rock Climb (post-game only — skip or Ursaring).

| Strategy | Slaves | Fly | Cut | Str | Wp | RS | RC | Zero moveset damage | Swap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Smeargle | 1 | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | **YES** | none |
| Furret + Farfetch'd | 1–2 | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | △ | **YES** | none |
| Farfetch'd | 1 | ✓ | ✓ | G | G | T/H | T/H | no | none |

| Dodrio swap | 0 | ✓ | T/H | G | G | T/H | T/H | no | Lapras → Doduo |

*G = Gyarados · T/H = Typhlosion/Heracross · ✓ = on a slave or already in a member's set · △ = Rock Climb post-game only (skip or Ursaring). Surf (Lapras) + Waterfall (Gyarados) are already in the optimal sets — never counted as damage.*

**Recommended:** 1 slave → grab the free Ilex **Farfetch'd** (Fly + Cut in one box slot). Optimal movesets → **Furret** (easy) or **Smeargle** (all 8, more setup).

## Phase 1 — New Bark → Violet City (lv 5 → ~12)

**Route:** Pick Cyndaquil, tutorial battle, Route 30/31, Violet City. Grind to ~10 before Falkner.

**Catch:**
- **Cyndaquil** — starter, minute 1. → Quilava at 14 (end of this phase).
- **Gastly** — Sprout Tower 1F–3F, **night only** (85%). Catch ONE. → Haunter at 25 (Phase 3).

**Grab:**
- **TM70 Flash** — Sprout Tower Elder, free after the tower.

**Moves to keep:**
- Cyndaquil: Tackle → Leer → Smokescreen(6) → **Ember(10)** → Quick Attack(13). **Keep Ember** — only Fire STAB until Flame Wheel at 20.
- Gastly: Hypnosis → Lick → Spite → Mean Look(8) → Curse(12). Hypnosis helps catching.

**Gym — Falkner (Flying):** Cyndaquil's Ember 2×s. Easy.

**SAVE:** In-game save at Violet Poké Center — creates the real `.sav`. Don't leave without it. Back up to OneDrive after.

## Phase 2 — Azalea → Goldenrod (lv 12 → ~20)

**Route:** Ilex Forest (Headbutt HM), Azalea trees for Heracross, Bugsy, Charcoal, Goldenrod.

**Catch:**
- **Heracross** — Azalea trees, 30% (lv 2–5). No evolution. Never misses.
- **Abra** — Goldenrod Game Corner, **200 coins** (lv 15). → Kadabra at 16 (evolve immediately). → Alakazam via trade.

**Grab:**
- **Charcoal** — Azalea kiln, after Bugsy → **Typhlosion** (Fire +20%).
- **TM52 Focus Blast** — Goldenrod Dept Store 5F, **5,500₽**. Buy now.
- TM27 **Return** — Dept Store **Sunday** lady (high-friendship lead). → Gyarados later.

**Buy (Goldenrod Dept Store):** Super Potion ×5 (700₽, 2F) · Great Ball ×10 (600₽) · Ultra Ball ×5 (1,200₽) · Super Repel ×5 (500₽) · Escape Rope ×2 (550₽). Skip 3F X-items, 4F vitamins (9,800₽), 6F drinks.

**Moves to keep:**
- Quilava (evolve at 14): **Flame Wheel(20)** replaces Ember.
- Heracross: Endure → Fury Attack(7) → Aerial Ace(13) → **Brick Break(19)**.
- Kadabra (evolve at 16): learns **Confusion** on evolution, then Disable(18).
- Gastly: Curse(12) → Night Shade(15) → **Confuse Ray(19)**.

**Gym:** Bugsy (Bug) — Quilava rolls. **Whitney (Normal)** — first real wall. Heracross Brick Break(19) or Kadabra Confusion.

## Phase 2.5 — The Trade Setup (lv ~17–22)

One-time, ~30–45 min. Goldenrod **Union Room** (2nd floor Pokécom Center). Trade Kadabra→Alakazam + Haunter→Gengar using a second melonDS instance. Full walkthrough in the HTML guide's Trade Setup reference.

- **Alakazam** — S-tier. 135 SpA + 120 Speed. The back half revolves around it.
- **Gengar** — A-tier. 130 SpA, Levitate (Ground immunity).

**SAVE:** Savestate slot 1 (F1) after both trades.

## Phase 3 — Ecruteak → Olivine (lv ~20 → 28)

**Route:** Morty, Kimono Girl (Surf), National Park, Route 38/39, Union Cave, Olivine.

**Catch:**
- **Lapras** — Union Cave B2F, **Friday only**. Enter from Violet side, surf to B2F pond. lv 20, one per week. **Don't KO or leave** — gone until next Friday.

**Grab:**
- **Quick Claw** — National Park → Heracross.
- **HM06 Rock Smash** — Hiker on Route 36.
- **TM30 Shadow Ball** — Morty's gym reward (free!) → Alakazam or Gengar.
- **Spell Tag** — Ecruteak City → Gengar (Ghost +20%).
- **HM03 Surf** — Dance Theater, Kimono Girl after Rocket grunt (post-Morty).
- **TM57 Charge Beam** — Olivine shore, right of the lighthouse → Alakazam.
- **Moomoo Milk** — Miltank farm Route 38/39 → buy a stack.

**Moves to keep:**
- Alakazam: Psybeam(24) → Reflect(28) → Recover(30) → **Calm Mind(36)** next phase. **Keep Recover**.
- Haunter→Gengar: Sucker Punch(22) → Haunter at 25 (learns **Shadow Punch**) → Payback(28) → **Shadow Ball(33)** next phase. **FREE — don't waste a TM.**
- Lapras (caught 20): Rain Dance(22) → Perish Song(27) → **Ice Beam(32)** next phase. **FREE.**
- Quilava: Swift(31) — filler.

**Gym:**
- **Morty (Ghost)** — Alakazam Confusion/Psychic (Psychic 2× Ghost/Poison). Clean sweep.
- **Chuck (Fighting)** — Alakazam / Gyarados. Watch Poliwrath's Surf.
- **Jasmine (Steel)** — Typhlosion/Quilava (Fire 2× Steel).

## Phase 4 — Cianwood → Lake of Rage (lv ~28 → 35)

**Route:** Cianwood (Chuck + Fly), SecretPotion → Olivine (Jasmine), Route 42 → Mahogany → Rocket HQ (Whirlpool) → Lake of Rage (free shiny Gyarados) → Pryce.

**Catch:**
- **Gyarados** — free shiny, Lake of Rage, lv 30. **Intimidate + Ice Fang(32)** useful from minute 1.

**Grab:**
- **HM02 Fly** — Cianwood, after Chuck. Nobody on the team learns Fly — the **HM strategy** comp you picked supplies the slave (default: free Ilex **Farfetch'd**, carries Fly + Cut).
- **HM04 Strength** — Hiker on Route 42.
- **TM65 Shadow Claw** — Route 42 → Gyarados.
- **HM05 Whirlpool** — Lance, Mahogany Rocket Hideout (for Lugia).
- **TM36 Sludge Bomb** — Route 43 guard, **after** Team Rocket leaves Mahogany → Gengar.
- **Choice Specs** — Lake of Rage (Surf, north-east) → **Alakazam** (Sp. Atk ×1.5).

**Buy (Cianwood Pharmacy — the only early Hyper Potion shop):** Hyper Potion ×5 (1,200₽) · Revive ×3 (1,500₽) · Full Heal ×3 (600₽). Regular marts gate Hyper Potions behind badge 5.

**Moves to keep — the team comes online:**
- **Typhlosion: Lava Plume(35)** → evolve at 36 → **Flamethrower(42)** next phase.
- **Gengar: Shadow Ball(33)** — free, main special Ghost move.
- **Lapras: Ice Beam(32)** — free, the Clair + Lance killer.
- **Gyarados: Ice Fang(32)** → Aqua Tail(35) → Rain Dance(38) → **Dragon Dance(44)** next phase.
- **Alakazam: Calm Mind(36)** — free setup.
- **Heracross: Close Combat(37)** — replaces Brick Break. Best Fighting move.

> **⚠️ Never spend coins on:** Shadow Ball (Gengar 33), Ice Beam (Lapras 32), Calm Mind (Alakazam 36), Psychic (Alakazam 40), Lava Plume (Typhlosion 35), Flamethrower (Typhlosion 42), Close Combat (Heracross 37), Ice Fang (Gyarados 32), Dragon Dance (Gyarados 44). The only TMs worth buying: **TM52 Focus Blast** (already in your bag) + **TM24 Thunderbolt** (Game Corner, optional).

**Gym — Pryce (Ice):** Typhlosion or Heracross. **Bench Gyarados** (4× weak to Ice). Heracross Close Combat 2×s.

## Phase 5 — Blackthorn → Victory Road (lv ~35 → 45+)

**Route:** Ice Path (Waterfall + Never-Melt Ice), Clair, then Victory Road (TM26 Earthquake) before the E4.

**Grab:**
- **HM07 Waterfall** — Ice Path (required for E4 + Lugia).
- **Never-Melt Ice** — Ice Path → Lapras (Ice +20%).
- **TM59 Dragon Pulse** — Clair's reward, Blackthorn Gym.
- **TM26 Earthquake** — Victory Road (pre-E4!) → Gyarados.

**Moves to keep:**
- **Typhlosion: Flamethrower(42)** — replaces Lava Plume.
- **Alakazam: Psychic(40)** — replaces Confusion/Psybeam. Debuffs Sp. Def 10%.
- **Gyarados: Dragon Dance(44)** — +1 Atk +1 Speed. Setup one, then sweep. **This is the E4 strategy.**

**Gym — Clair (Dragon):** Lapras Ice Beam (Kingdra 2× weak, Lapras 130 base HP to tank). Give Lapras Never-Melt Ice.

**SAVE:** Save state (F1) at Blackthorn Poké Center before Victory Road.

## Phase 6 — Elite Four (lv ~45, E4 levels 42–50)

**Counters:**
- **Will (Psychic)** — Gengar Shadow Ball (Ghost 2× Psychic).
- **Koga (Poison)** — Alakazam Psychic (2× Poison). Calm Mind vs Muk first.
- **Bruno (Fighting)** — Alakazam Psychic / Gyarados Waterfall.
- **Karen (Dark)** — Heracross Close Combat (2× Dark). Gengar Shadow Ball for Houndoom.
- **Lance (Dragon)** — **Lapras Ice Beam** (4× Dragonite). Gyarados Ice Fang backup.

Lance's team (Gyarados → Dragonite ×3 → Charizard → Aerodactyl) is all Flying-typed — Ice 4× on Dragonite, 2× on the rest.

**Optional — Lugia swap:** Whirl Islands, lv 45, needs Whirlpool. Swap in for Typhlosion (Fire is dead weight at E4).
